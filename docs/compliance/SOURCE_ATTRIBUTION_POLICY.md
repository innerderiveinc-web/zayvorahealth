# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Source Attribution Policy

**Status:** Active Clinical Policy  
**Applies to:** Clinical Engines, Knowledge Ingestion, Hakeem Sahab AI™, Content Editors  

---

## 1. Guiding Principles

1. **Clear Distinction Between Literature & Platform:** A citation of classical literature, a pharmacopoeia monograph, or a university trial does not imply that the cited institution endorses, owns, or operates ZAYVORA Health OS™.
2. **Mandatory Categorization:** Every external reference must specify its source category (`HISTORICAL_REFERENCE`, `ACADEMIC_REFERENCE`, `EVIDENCE_SOURCE`, `PUBLICATION_SOURCE`, `REGULATORY_SOURCE`, `EXAMPLE_ONLY`).
3. **No Implied Affiliation:** Academic credentials of attending clinicians (e.g. BUMS from Tibbia College or MD from AMU) must be displayed solely as personal educational qualifications, never as institutional partnerships of ZAYVORA Matab™.

---

## 2. Citations Formatting Standard

When Hakeem Sahab AI™ or the ZAYVORA Knowledge Intelligence™ platform references literature:
- **Classical Books:** Cite author and text (e.g. *Ibn Sina, Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb, Book II*).
- **National Pharmacopoeias:** Cite official standard (e.g. *Unani Pharmacopoeia of India, Part I, Vol. II*).
- **Research Studies:** Cite author, journal, DOI (e.g. *Alam et al., J. Ethnopharmacol., 2021*).
- **Clinician Bios:** State degree and granting university, followed by current appointment: *“Senior Consulting Tabeeb at ZAYVORA Matab™”*.
