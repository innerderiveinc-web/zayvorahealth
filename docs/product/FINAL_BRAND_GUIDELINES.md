## Prohibited Claims

The following phrases are **banned** unless backed by verifiable evidence:

- ❌ "Ministry approved" (unless CDSCO/AYUSH approval obtained)
- ❌ "100% safe" (no herb is universally safe)
- ❌ "Guaranteed cure" (no therapy guarantees outcomes)
- ❌ "Clinically validated" (unless peer-reviewed RCT published)
- ❌ "Certified secure" (no security certification obtained)
- ❌ "Production ready" (system is pre-staging)
- ❌ "Compliant" without specifying the standard and evidence

## System Status Disclosure

Every patient-facing and clinician-facing page must display the current honest system status:

**Current Status: Pre-staging / Pilot Candidate**
