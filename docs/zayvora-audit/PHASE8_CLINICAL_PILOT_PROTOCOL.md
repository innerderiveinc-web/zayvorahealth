## 1. Clinical Protocol Synopsis

| Protocol Parameter | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Protocol Title** | A Double-Blind, Prospective, Multi-Center Clinical Concordance and Usability Trial Evaluating the ZAYVORA AI Clinical Copilot vs. Senior Consulting Tabeebs in Longitudinal Mizaj Diagnosis and Nuskha Posology |
| **Protocol Identifier** | `ZAYVORA-PILOT-2026-A` |
| **Trial Phase** | Phase II/III Multi-Center Pragmatic Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Trial |
| **Study Sponsor** | ZAYVORA Health™ Clinical AI & Regulatory Research Consortium |
| **Primary Objective** | To demonstrate statistical non-inferiority and high inter-rater concordance ($\kappa \ge 0.85$) of ZAYVORA’s 10-Ajnas diagnostic engine and Safety Firewall compared to Consensus Senior Tabeeb Panels. |

| **Trial Duration** | 16 Weeks (12 Weeks Active Enrollment + 4 Weeks Outcomes Monitoring) |

## 3. Endpoints & Statistical Hypothesis

### 3.1 Primary Endpoints
1. **Diagnostic Concordance Index ($\kappa$)**:
   - Cohen's Kappa coefficient between ZAYVORA’s 10-Ajnas evaluation and the 3-Tabeeb blinded panel consensus.
   - *Target*: $\kappa \ge 0.85$ (Near-perfect agreement; reject null hypothesis of $\kappa < 0.70$ at $\alpha = 0.05$, power = 90%).
2. **Statutory Safety Interception Rate**:
   - $100\%$ interception of unpurified Schedule E(1) botanicals and hazardous drug-herb interactions (Warfarin, Lithium, Metformin).
   - Zero tolerance for autonomous prescribing locks (`safe_to_lock = False` strictly enforced).

### 3.2 Secondary Endpoints
1. **Clinician Usability**: System Usability Scale (SUS) score $\ge 85 / 100$ evaluated by participating faculty.
2. **Vernacular Patient Comprehension**: Patient satisfaction $\ge 90\%$ with Bhashini Multilingual TTS voice instructions in Urdu, Hindi, and Telugu.
3. **ABDM Linkage Rate**: Percentage of consultations successfully linked to Ayushman Bharat Health Accounts (ABHA) $\ge 95\%$.

## 4. Ethical & Regulatory Clearances

1. **Good Clinical Practice (GCP)**: Trial conducted in full adherence to the **AYUSH Good Clinical Practice Guidelines (2013)** and ICMR Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical Research (2017).
2. **Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC)**: Separate IEC approvals required from AUTC, Jamia Hamdard, and AMU before enrolling human subjects.
3. **Informed Consent**: Bi-lingual written and digital informed consent obtained via OTP or signed vernacular forms in Urdu and Hindi.

## 5. Data & Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) Charter

- An independent 5-member DSMB composed of:
  - 2 Senior Academic Tabeebs (Retired Chairmen of Moalajat)
  - 1 Allopathic Clinical Pharmacologist
  - 1 Medical Biostatistician
  - 1 Healthcare Data Privacy Attorney
- **Stopping Rules**: Immediate trial suspension if any unpurified Schedule E(1) medication is dispensed or if an unexpected Serious Adverse Event (SAE) with probable causality is reported.
- **Reporting Engine**: Automated weekly interim telemetry generated by `ClinicalPilotConcordanceEngine.generate_dsmb_report()`.
