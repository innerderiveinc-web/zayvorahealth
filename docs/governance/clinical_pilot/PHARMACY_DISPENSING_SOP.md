# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Pharmacy FEFO Dispensing & Mudabbar SOP

**SOP Reference:** SOP-PHARM-001  

## 1. Dispensing Principles
1. First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO): Always pick medication lots in order of ascending expiration date.
2. Schedule E(1) Statutory Gate: Never dispense uncertified toxic botanicals; verify batch Mudabbar certificate.
3. Cold Chain Compliance: Confirm storage temperatures prior to packaging syrups and oils.
4. Electronic Reconciliation: Record dispensing event digitally to trigger stock decrement and invoice creation.
