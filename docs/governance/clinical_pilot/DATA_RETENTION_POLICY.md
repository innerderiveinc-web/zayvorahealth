# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Data Retention & Archival Policy

**Policy Reference:** ZAY-POL-DATA-01  

## 1. Retention Durations
- Outpatient Clinical Encounters: Retained for a minimum of ten (10) years from date of visit.
- Pediatric Medical Records: Retained until the patient reaches twenty-one (21) years of age, or ten years, whichever is longer.
- Prescription Fulfillment Logs: Retained for minimum two (2) years under D&C Rule 65.
- Cryptographic Audit Ledger: Permanently retained in immutable write-once read-many (WORM) storage.
