# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Clinical Pilot Protocol (Draft for Institutional Review)

**Protocol Number:** ZAY-PILOT-2026-01  
**Study Title:** Prospective Multi-Center Clinical Evaluation of ZAYVORA Health OS™ in Outpatient Unani Clinical Practice  
**Status:** DRAFT — Awaiting Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) / IRB Review & Approval  
**Date:** September 16, 2026  

## 1. Pilot Scope & Constraints
- **Maximum Sites:** Strictly limited to three (3) designated university teaching hospitals or institutional Unani outpatient departments.
- **Patient Cohort Limit:** Maximum 500 active outpatients across all three participating centers.
- **Study Duration:** Six (6) months from first patient enrollment.
- **Investigator Eligibility:** Licensed CCIM/AYUSH registered medical practitioners only.

## 2. Objectives
- Primary: Evaluate clinical documentation accuracy, physician usability, and safety firewall compliance.
- Secondary: Measure diagnostic concordance of 10-Ajnas Mizaj classification and longitudinal Afaal cascade progression.

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop AI Mandate
- Hakeem Sahab™ AI operates strictly as an assistive clinical co-pilot.
- All diagnoses, prescriptions, and encounter sign-offs require manual, un-delegated physician review.
- Autonomous AI prescription signing or encounter locking is strictly prohibited by software architecture.
