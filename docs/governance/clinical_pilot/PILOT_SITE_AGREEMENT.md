# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Pilot Site Agreement (Template)

**Agreement Title:** Multi-Center Institutional Pilot Site Agreement  
**Status:** DRAFT — Pending Institutional Review  

## 1. Participating Parties
This Agreement is entered into between ZAYVORA Healthcare Technologies and the Participating Pilot Institution (University Hospital / Teaching Institute).

## 2. Institutional Commitments
1. The Institution shall assign qualified, CCIM-registered clinicians to oversee all outpatient encounters.
2. The Institution shall ensure all dispensing takes place via an authorized pharmacy holding valid state drug retail licenses.
3. The Institution shall limit patient enrollment to a maximum of 500 total active patients across all pilot departments.
4. The Institution shall immediately notify ZAYVORA of any Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) within 24 hours.
