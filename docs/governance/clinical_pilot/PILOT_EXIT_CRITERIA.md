# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Clinical Pilot Exit & Suspension Criteria

**Document Reference:** ZAY-GOV-EXIT-01  

## 1. Suspension Triggers (Emergency Halt)
The pilot shall be immediately suspended if:
- Any preventable Severe Adverse Event (SAE) directly attributable to software posology recommendation occurs.
- A critical security breach exposing real patient PHI is identified.
- Clinical Concordance drops below 85% across consecutive monthly audits.

## 2. Successful Exit Criteria
The pilot shall be deemed successfully completed when:
- 500 patients have completed their acute/chronic follow-up continuum.
- Zero unresolved critical safety incidents recorded.
- Formal DSMB sign-off report issued to participating institutional review boards.
