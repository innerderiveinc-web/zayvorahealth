## Executive Statutory Mandate

Per the **Drugs and Cosmetics Act (1940)**, Schedule E(1), and the **Unani Pharmacopoeia of India (UPI)**:
> **STATUTORY INVARIANT**: No crude or unpurified (*Kham*) botanical or mineral statutory poison may be compounded into any commercial SKU or prescribed in any clinical Nuskha. ## 1. Suranjan Talkh (Colchicum autumnale L.) Detoxification SOP

- **Substance Code**: `SCH-E1-001`
- **Target Chemical Transformation**: Partial conversion of toxic free alkaloid colchicine into safer desacetyl-colchicine derivatives; reduction of corrosive gastrointestinal mucotoxicity.
- **Classical Reference**: *Kitab-al-Taksheer wa Tadbeer-e-Adviyah*; *Qarabadin-e-Qadri*.

### Procedural Steps
1. **Raw Material Inspection**: Sound, dry, mature corms free from fungal molds and insect boring.
2. **Coarse Slicing**: Sliced into uniform 2-3 mm cross-sections using stainless steel surgical blades.
3. **Milk Steeping (Sheer-e-Gao)**:
   - Sliced corms immersed in fresh pasteurized cow's milk (*Sheer-e-Gao*) in a 1:4 (weight/volume) ratio.
   - Kept under controlled ambient temperature (20–25°C) for 7 consecutive days.
   - **Critical Process Parameter (CPP)**: Milk must be decanted and replaced with fresh milk every 24 hours to prevent acidification and bacterial fermentation.
4. **Washing & Desiccation**:
   - On the 8th day, corms removed, washed thoroughly with lukewarm distilled water until effluent is clear.
   - Spread in thin layers in a dehumidified drying chamber at 40°C until moisture content is $< 8.0\%$.
5. **Analytical Holding Point (IPQC Gate)**:
   - **TLC Fingerprint**: Single prominent spot with standard colchicine ($R_f \approx 0.42$, solvent system: chloroform-acetone-diethylamine 5:4:1).
   - **Quantitative HPLC Assay**: Colchicine content verified between $0.20\% - 0.40\% \text{ w/w}$ (excess unpurified levels $> 0.60\%$ fail the batch).

## 2. Azaraqi / Kuchla (Strychnos nux-vomica L.) Detoxification SOP

- **Substance Code**: `SCH-E1-002`
- **Target Chemical Transformation**: Hydrolytic breakdown and thermal degradation of lethal strychnine and brucine alkaloids into less toxic N-oxide and isostrychnine derivatives.
- **Classical Reference**: *Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb*; *Bayaz-e-Kabeer*.

### Procedural Steps
1. **Raw Seed Pre-selection**: Button-shaped, silky-sheened mature seeds of minimum diameter 18 mm.
2. **Steeping Phase**:
   - Immersed in fresh cow's urine (*Aab-e-Gao*) or fermented rice gruel (*Kanji*) for 7 full days.
   - Urine replaced daily at 09:00 AM UTC. Seeds soften and expand by ~40% volume.
3. **Mechanical De-cuticulation & Embryo Extraction**:
   - Softened seeds removed; outer epidermal cuticle (*Post-e-Bairuni*) scraped completely using a blunt brass knife.
   - Seed split longitudinally along the median raphe; the embryonic radicle and cotyledon (*Fals-e-Mani / Zehra-e-Kuchla*) carefully excised and discarded.
4. **Thermal Processing (Bhon-na)**:
   - De-embryonated cotyledons washed with water, boiled in cow's milk for 3 hours using a Dolayantra apparatus.
   - Cooled, sliced into flakes, and lightly fried in clarified cow butter (*Roghan-e-Zard / Ghee*) until light golden brown.
5. **Analytical Holding Point (IPQC Gate)**:
   - **Total Alkaloids (Strychnine + Brucine)**: Must measure $\le 1.80\% \text{ w/w}$.
   - **Strychnine/Brucine Ratio**: Checked via reverse-phase HPLC at 254 nm. Free toxic brucine must not exceed $0.65\%$.

## 3. Bish / Meetha Telia (Aconitum ferox Wall.) Detoxification SOP

- **Substance Code**: `SCH-E1-003`
- **Target Chemical Transformation**: Pyrolytic and hydrolytic conversion of cardiotoxic diester alkaloids (aconitine, mesaconitine) into relatively benign monoester benzoylaconine and non-toxic aconine.

### Procedural Steps
1. **Selection & Washing**: Thick tuberous roots washed in cold running water to eliminate soil and adherent particulates.
2. **Gomutra Steeping**:
   - Immersed in earthen jars containing fresh cow's urine.
   - Kept in sunlight for 7 days, replacing liquid daily.
3. **Milk Decocting (Swedana)**:
   - Tubers tied in a clean muslin cloth pouch (*Pottali*) and suspended in a Dolayantra vessel filled with cow's milk.
   - Boiled continuously for 3 hours, keeping tubers immersed above the vessel base.
4. **Peeling & Slicing**:
   - Outer brown epidermal sheath peeled off immediately while warm.
   - Sliced into 1 mm chips and dried under solar shade.
5. **Analytical Holding Point (IPQC Gate)**:
   - **Aconitine Content**: HPLC assay must confirm total diester diterpenoid alkaloids $< 0.02\% \text{ w/w}$ (crude material typically contains $0.3\% - 0.8\%$).
   - Absence of refractory ventricular arrhythmogenic threshold in biological assay.

## 4. Bhilawa (Semecarpus anacardium L.f.) Detoxification SOP

- **Substance Code**: `SCH-E1-004`
- **Target Chemical Transformation**: Extraction and neutralization of vesicant urushiol-like phenolic resins (bhilawanols) from the fruit pericarps.

### Procedural Steps
1. **Decapitation**: Persistent fleshy cup-shaped receptacles (thalami) excised and discarded.
2. **Brick Powder Adsorption**:
   - Ripe fruits slit with cross-incisions and placed into a coarse jute gunny sack containing crushed sun-dried red brick powder (*Ishtak-e-Kham* / *Geru*).
   - Sack manually or mechanically rubbed for 4 hours; the porous brick powder absorbs the dark, caustic pericarp oil.
3. **Washing & Milk Boiling**:
   - Fruits extracted from powder and washed with hot water.
   - Boiled in cow milk for 2 hours in a closed extraction vessel.
4. **Final Desiccation**: Dried in vacuum tray dryer at 45°C for 24 hours.
5. **Analytical Holding Point (IPQC Gate)**:
   - Dermal patch test on guinea pig skin negative for acute chemical blistering.
   - Phenolic resin yield $< 2.5\%$.
