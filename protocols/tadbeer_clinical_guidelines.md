*Evidence-Based Classical Unani Physical Medicine & Safety Guardrails (Master Architecture Horizon 2)*

## 1. Clinical Scope & Foundational Tenet

*Ilaj-bit-Tadbeer* (Regimenal Therapy) constitutes the second tier of the Unani therapeutic hierarchy:

$$\text{Ilaj-bil-Ghiza (Dietetics)} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{Ilaj-bit-Tadbeer (Regimens)}} \longrightarrow \text{Ilaj-bil-Dawa (Pharmacotherapy)} \longrightarrow \text{Ilaj-bil-Yad (Surgery)}$$

The primary mechanism is **Tanqiya-e-Mawad** (Evacuation/Elimination of Morbid Humoral Waste) and **Ta'deel-e-Mizaj** (Harmonization of Temperament), relieving internal organ congestion and activating the innate self-healing faculty (*Tabi'at*).

## 2. The 12 Canonical Modalities

### 1. Hijama (Cupping Therapy)
- **Modality Types**:
  - **Hijama bil Shart (Wet Cupping / Bloodletting)**: Targeted superficial scarification of skin with negative pressure suction.
  - **Hijama bila Shart (Dry Cupping)**: Vacuum suction without incision for counter-irritation, diversion (*Imala*), and localized hyperemia.
  - **Hijama Nariyya (Fire Cupping)**: Thermal negative pressure induction.
- **Key Indications**: *Waja-ul-Mafasil* (Arthritis), *Niqras* (Gout), *Irq-un-Nasa* (Sciatica), *Suda* (Tension cephalea), localized blood stasis (*Ghalba-e-Dam*).
- **Absolute Contraindications**: Severe anemia ($\text{Hb} < 9.0 \text{ g/dL}$), hemophilia / anticoagulant therapy (INR > 1.5), thrombocytopenia ($< 100,000/\mu\text{L}$), pregnancy (abdomen/lumbar region), acute active infection/cellulitis.
- **Standard Schedule**: 15–25 mmHg vacuum, 3–5 minutes suction, 3–7 superficial incisions ($\le 0.5 \text{ mm}$ depth). Maximum 4 cups per session. Minimum interval: 14 to 28 days.

### 2. Fasd (Venesection / Phlebotomy)
- **Mechanism**: Systemic evacuation (*Tanqiya-e-Kulliyyah*) of plethoric, combusted sanguineous humor directly from superficial veins (Basilic / *Basilique*, Cephalic / *Qifal*, Median cubital / *Ak-hal*).
- **Key Indications**: *Imtila-e-Dam* (Sanguineous plethora), acute hypertensive crises with stroke risk in robust constitution, chronic severe migraine with venous fullness.
- **Absolute Contraindications**: Debilitated geriatrics ($> 70$ yrs), pediatric patients ($< 14$ yrs), extreme asthenia (*Zuf-e-Qalb / Zuf-e-Aza-e-Raeesa*), active hypotension (BP $< 100/60$).
- **Volume Limits**: Maximum single blood draw: $150 - 300 \text{ mL}$ depending on patient weight and vital baseline.

### 3. Dalak (Therapeutic Medical Massage)
- **Modality Types**:
  - *Dalak-e-Sulb* (Hard/Deep Friction): Disperses thickened indurations, stimulates tonicity.
  - *Dalak-e-Layyin* (Gentle/Effleurage): Soothing, sedative, relieves fatigue and insomnia.
  - *Dalak-e-Kaseer* (Prolonged): Reduces localized adipose plethora.
  - *Dalak-e-Mu'tadil* (Moderate): Fortifies *Hararat-e-Ghariziyyah* and muscular trophic tone.
- **Vehicles (*Adhan*)**: *Roghan-e-Balsan*, *Roghan-e-Surkh*, *Roghan-e-Banafsha*, *Roghan-e-Zaitoon*.
- **Duration**: 20–45 minutes per treatment session.

### 4. Hammam (Therapeutic Medicinal Bath)
- **Chambers**: *Bait-ul-Awwal* (Cold/Antechamber) $\to$ *Bait-ul-Thani* (Warm/Tepidarium) $\to$ *Bait-ul-Thalith* (Hot/Calidarium).
- **Indications**: Dissolution of dense humors (*Tahleel-e-Akhlat-e-Ghaleeza*), softening of dry skin, metabolic stimulation.
- **Contraindications**: Acute febrile illnesses (*Hummiyat-e-Haddah*), decompensated cardiac failure, aortic stenosis.

### 5. Natool (Irrigation / Douching / Shirodhara-Analogue)
- **Technique**: Controlled, continuous pouring of medicated decoctions or oils over the head or afflicted joints from a calibrated height (15–30 cm).
- **Indications**: *Sahar* (Severe Insomnia), *Malikhuliya* (Melancholic Depression), *Suda-e-Safrawi* (Bilious Cephalea).
- **Formulations**: Decoction of *Khashkhash*, *Gul-e-Banafsha*, *Kahu*, *Baboona*.

### 6. Riyazat (Prescribed Exercise)
- **Prescription**: Categorized into Mild (*Khafeef*), Moderate (*Mu'tadil*), and Vigorous (*Qawi*).
- **Clinical Rule**: Must precede meals; never performed on a full stomach or during acute metabolic exacerbations.

### 7. Huqna (Medicated Enema)
- **Modality Types**:
  - *Huqna-e-Layyinah* (Soothing/Nutritive enema with milk and mucilage).
  - *Huqna-e-Mus-hilah* (Purgative enema with castor oil, Cassia fistula, and salt).
  - *Huqna-e-Daf-e-Taffun* (Antiseptic/Anti-inflammatory enema for colitis).

### 8. Irsal-e-Alaq (Hirudotherapy / Leeching)
- **Target**: Microcirculatory stasis, localized venous insufficiency, osteoarthritic joint effusion, non-healing vascular ulcers.
- **Medicinal Species**: *Hirudo medicinalis* (certified farm-bred, single-use, sterile).
- **Precautions**: Strict bleeding time, clotting time, and platelet count verification.

### 9. Qay (Therapeutic Emesis)
- Evacuation of morbid phlegm and bilious humors accumulated in the upper gastrointestinal tract (*Fuzalat-e-Meda*).

### 10. Ishal (Therapeutic Purgation)
- Systemic downward evacuation using classical compound decoctions (*Joshanda Munzij wa Mus-hil*).

### 11. Tareeq (Diaphoresis / Medicated Sweating)
- Facilitating perspiration through steam, warm blankets, and diaphoretic teas to clear superficial capillary toxemia.

### 12. Kai (Cauterization)
- Historical surgical intervention; reserved in modern clinical operating protocols strictly for specified non-responsive deep neuropathic conditions under sterile surgical conditions.

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Procedure Assessment Protocol

Before executing any invasive or evacutive regimen (Hijama, Fasd, Huqna, Irsal-e-Alaq), the clinician must verify:
1. **Vital Signs Baseline**: Heart Rate, Blood Pressure (Systolic & Diastolic), Respiratory Rate, SpO2.
2. **Coagulation & Hematology Profile**: Hemoglobin $\ge 10.0 \text{ g/dL}$, Platelet Count $\ge 120,000/\mu\text{L}$, INR $< 1.3$.
3. **Mizaj Compatibility**: Patients with extreme *Barid Yabis* (Cold-Dry) cachexia or severe debility must receive restorative nutrition (*Muqawwiyat*) before undergoing evacutive regimens.
4. **Electronic Consent**: Signed statutory patient consent record linked in the EMR.
