# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Human Oversight Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**SOP Reference:** SOP-CLIN-001  
**Effective Date:** September 16, 2026  

## 1. Purpose
To define the mandatory procedure for human physician review, verification, and cryptographic signing of all digital encounters.

## 2. Procedure
1. The clinician accesses the outpatient queue and opens the patient encounter cockpit.
2. The clinician independently records subjective symptoms, physical examination (Nabz, Lisan, Baul), and vitals.
3. If AI decision support is queried, the clinician must critically inspect all 5 response layers.
4. The clinician authors the final Nuskha prescription, ensuring posology and dietary parhez instructions are tailored to the patient's individual Mizaj.
5. The clinician enters their CCIM credentials and digital PIN to seal the encounter. Post-signing edits are immutably locked.
