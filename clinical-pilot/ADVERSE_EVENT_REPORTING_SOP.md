# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Adverse Event & Pharmacovigilance SOP

**SOP Reference:** SOP-PHARM-002  

## 1. Purpose & Regulatory Alignment
To ensure systematic recording and statutory reporting of Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) in compliance with the National Pharmacovigilance Programme for ASU&H Drugs (NPvPY).

## 2. Reporting Workflow
1. Identification: Any unintended or noxious response occurring at doses normally used in clinical practice.
2. Digital Incident Entry: Clinician or Pharmacist submits an ADR report via .
3. Causality Assessment: The Clinical Governance Board reviews causality using the WHO-UMC causality assessment scale.
4. Statutory Dispatch: Severe adverse events reported to the State Pharmacovigilance Centre within 24 hours.
