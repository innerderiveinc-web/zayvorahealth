# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — User Access Control & Periodic Review SOP

**SOP Reference:** SOP-SEC-003  

## 1. Access Control Principles
- Least Privilege: Users are granted access strictly necessary for their clinical/operational duties.
- Role Segregation: Non-clinical users (receptionists, accountants) are barred from clinical encounter charts.

## 2. Periodic Review Schedule
- Monthly: Review and revocation of accounts for clinicians or staff whose institutional affiliation has ended.
- Quarterly: Formal audit log review and privilege reconciliation by the CISO.
