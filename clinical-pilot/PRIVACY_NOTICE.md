# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Patient Privacy Notice

**Compliance:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPB 2023), HIPAA Privacy Rule  

## 1. What Data We Collect
- Demographics (Name, age, gender, contact number).
- Clinical health records (Vitals, symptoms, Mizaj classifications, prescriptions, lab results).

## 2. How We Protect Your Data
- All sensitive medical fields are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM.
- Access is restricted through fine-grained Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- Digital records are anchored to a tamper-evident cryptographic audit ledger.
