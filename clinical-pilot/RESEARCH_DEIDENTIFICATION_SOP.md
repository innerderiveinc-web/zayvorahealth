# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Research Cohort De-Identification SOP

**SOP Reference:** SOP-RES-001  

## 1. Safe Harbor De-Identification Protocol
Prior to exporting clinical records for research or RWE analysis:
- Strip all direct identifiers: Patient name, phone number, email, national identity numbers, exact residential address.
- Transform dates to relative day offsets or year only.
- Pseudonymize MRN into random cryptographic study subject ID ().
