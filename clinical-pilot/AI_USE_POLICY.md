# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Artificial Intelligence Use & Safety Policy

**Policy Reference:** ZAY-POL-AI-01  
**Status:** DRAFT — Mandatory Clinical AI Governance  

## 1. Non-Autonomous Decision Support Boundary
Hakeem Sahab™ AI is classified as a Clinical Decision Support (CDS) tool under FDA SaMD guidance and WHO guidelines on AI in Health.
- The AI **SHALL NEVER** independently generate, approve, or lock a prescription.
- The AI **SHALL NEVER** override clinical judgement or dismiss a clinician's dosage selection.
- All AI responses must enforce  across all payload layers.

## 2. Emergency Escalation
Any query containing emergency biomedical red flag symptoms (acute crushing chest pain, signs of stroke, acute abdomen, anaphylaxis) triggers an immediate clinical hold () directing urgent hospital transfer.
