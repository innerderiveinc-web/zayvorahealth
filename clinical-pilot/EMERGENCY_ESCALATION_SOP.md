# ZAYVORA Health OS™ — Emergency Red Flag Escalation SOP

**SOP Reference:** SOP-CLIN-002  

## 1. Emergency Red Flag Triggers
- Cardiovascular: Crushing chest pain, radiation to arm/jaw, severe dyspnea, diaphoresis.
- Neurological: Sudden hemiparesis, facial droop, acute slurred speech, sudden loss of consciousness.
- Abdominal: Board-like rigidity, hematemesis, severe peritonitis signs.
- Obstetric: Severe pre-eclampsia symptoms, active third-trimester hemorrhage.

## 2. Escalation Action
1. The software Deterministic Safety Firewall immediately halts outpatient herbal recommendations and sets .
2. The system displays an emergency red banner with local emergency contact protocols.
3. On-duty clinical staff must arrange immediate transport to the nearest tertiary critical care emergency department.
